OFFICIAL RACE RULES

01. Follow all moderator instructions.
02. No intentional ramming or sabotaging other racers.
03. No shortcuts or leaving the designated track.
04. Exploiting, cheating or using glitches for an advantage is prohibited.
05. Do not interfere with other racers.
06. Respect all participants and moderators.
07. All racers must complete the required 3 laps.
08. Do not argue with moderators during the race.
09. Report rule violations to the moderators.
10. Moderators have the authority to disqualify participants who break the rules.
11. Go-Kart tuning is allowed, including horsepower adjustments.
12. Mass rule-breaking or repeated serious violations may result in a ban from participating in future community events.
